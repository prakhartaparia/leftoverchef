# Note

**AI tools used:** ChatGPT, OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity.

**What the team changed:** Initially, the app did not display nutritional or cost estimates. We later added these features to make the app more useful for diet-conscious and budget-conscious users. While the estimates are not guaranteed to be 100% accurate, they provide users with a helpful starting point to make more conscious, informed decisions about the recipes they choose. We wanted to make an effort to consider not just food waste, but also users' nutritional and budget needs.
