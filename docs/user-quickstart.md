# User Quickstart

1. Open `index.html` in a browser. A first-visit guided intro highlights the main sections and controls; use **Next** to continue, **Skip tour** to dismiss it, or **Finish** to close it. It appears once per browser; use the small **Tour** button beside the theme icon to replay it later. Vegetarian recipes are shown by default.
2. Select ingredients you have. Results use only selected ingredients and can use a subset of your selection. Salt, oil, water, and common spices are assumed kitchen essentials. Selecting eggs, chicken, fish, or prawns switches results to non-veg recipes. Mark anything close to expiry as **Use soon**.
3. Choose **Find my recipe**. Sort matches by score, nutrition, estimated cost, serving size, prep time, cook time, difficulty, or use-soon ingredients.
4. Review recipe instructions, separate prep and cook times, advance-prep notes, estimated nutrition and cost, estimated food value used, and the zero-waste score with its ingredient-count calculation. **Cook with me** opens guided steps; **Browse kitchen tips** opens storage ideas.

For your own three-ingredient search, switch on **3-item mode** beside the basket count and select exactly three ingredients. You can also choose **3-ingredient match** in Judge Quick Checks to load the sample path. Results use exactly the three selected ingredients or show a clear no-match message. Choose **Standard match** to return to regular matching. When two are selected, optional compatible suggestions may appear; choose one only if you already have it. If no sample recipe includes all three, the app explains that there is no match. The dark-mode control is in the top-right corner. Refresh the page to clear the current selection and reset the theme.

Nutrition, cost, and savings are local illustrative estimates, not personalized advice or live prices.
